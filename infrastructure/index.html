Lunar communications

Which capabilities have flown, and which remain planned? These three records document a direct optical link, a far-side relay and a proposed shared relay service. Each status carries the date supported by its source.

LADEE laser communications experiment

Mission ended · 2014-04-18

The spacecraft reached the lunar surface at the end of its mission. The measured rates describe the experiment.

Evidence and sources
  • NASA measured a 622 Mbps optical downlink from lunar orbit and a 20 Mbps uplink from New Mexico to LADEE. NASA, 2013-10-22
  • NASA confirmed the planned impact of the host spacecraft in April 2014. NASA, 2014-04-18

Queqiao-2

Mission use documented · 2024-06-02

The cited mission reports establish service for named spacecraft. Current uptime, spare capacity and terms for another customer remain unverified.

Evidence and sources
  • CNSA reported a 24-hour elliptical lunar orbit and a successful communications test with Chang’e-4 on the far side. CNSA, 2024-04-12
  • Queqiao-2 supported the Chang’e-6 landing on the lunar far side on 2 June 2024. CNSA, 2024-06-02

Lunar Pathfinder

Planned service · 2026-10-12

The published schedule depends on launch, lunar deployment and commissioning. Service entry and customer capacity remain unverified.

Evidence and sources
  • ESA reported final environmental testing in the United Kingdom and a planned 2027 launch. The mission design calls for a relay in an elliptical lunar orbit, with LuSEE-Night among the intended users. ESA, 2026-10-12
